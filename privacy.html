Privacy Policy

Short version: Receep does not collect your invoices. Everything you import is processed and stored on your iPhone. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no analytics SDK.

1. Who we are

Receep (the “App”, Chinese name 本机票夹) is an independent iOS app for keeping receipts and invoices locally. For privacy questions, contact sunjun20141@qq.com.

2. What the App stores on your device

  • The invoice files you import (PDF or images) and the copies the App archives.
  • Fields extracted from those invoices, such as seller, invoice number, date, amount, currency, and category.
  • App settings, including face lock, export watermark, auto-archive, default export format, and your purchase unlock state.
  • Export files you create (Excel or PDF), saved in the App’s Documents folder.

This data lives in the App’s sandbox on your device. It is not uploaded to us or to any third party.

3. Recognition and processing

Text recognition (OCR) runs entirely on device using Apple’s Vision framework. Invoice images and recognized text are not sent to any server.

4. Permissions

PermissionWhy it is used
Photos / FilesOnly when you choose invoices to import. The system picker gives the App access to the items you select.
Face ID / Touch IDOptional app lock. Authentication is performed by iOS; biometric data stays in the Secure Enclave and is never available to the App.
Files app accessLets you view or copy exported files through the system Files app.

5. Purchases

The one-time in-app purchase is processed by Apple. We receive only the transaction result needed to unlock features. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the purchase.

6. Network access

The App does not upload invoices. Network access is used only to load App Store product information, verify or restore purchases through StoreKit, and open this policy or the Terms of Use in a browser when you tap those links.

7. No tracking, no ads

The App contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share personal data.

8. Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can clear the receipt box in Settings. Deleting the App removes its local data, including archived originals and export files stored inside the App’s folder.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — in fact, we do not collect personal information at all.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected before they take effect.

11. Contact

Email: sunjun20141@qq.com

隐私政策

一句话版本：Receep 不收集你的发票。所有导入的内容都在你的 iPhone 上完成识别和存储，没有账号、没有云同步、没有统计 SDK。

一、我们是谁

Receep（本机票夹）是一款独立开发的 iOS 应用，用于在本机保存和整理票据。隐私相关问题请联系 sunjun20141@qq.com。

二、App 在本机保存哪些数据

  • 你导入的发票文件（PDF 或图片）以及 App 归档的副本。
  • 从发票中识别出的字段，例如销售方、发票号码、开票日期、金额、币种和类别。
  • App 设置，包括面容锁、导出水印、按月归档、默认导出格式，以及你的买断解锁状态。
  • 你主动生成的导出文件（Excel 或 PDF），保存在 App 的 Documents 目录。

这些数据只存在于你设备上的 App 沙盒中，不会上传给我们或任何第三方。

三、识别与处理

文字识别（OCR）完全在本机通过 Apple Vision 框架完成，发票图片和识别结果不会被发送到任何服务器。

四、系统权限

权限用途
照片 / 文件仅在你选择要导入的发票时使用。系统选择器只会把选中的项目交给 App。
面容 ID / 触控 ID可选的应用锁。验证由 iOS 完成，生物特征数据保存在 Secure Enclave 中，App 无法读取。
文件 App 访问让你可以在系统「文件」App 中查看或复制导出的文件。

五、购买

一次买断的内购由 Apple 处理。我们只收到用于解锁功能的交易结果，不会获取或保存你的支付信息。购买行为适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

六、网络访问

App 不会上传发票。网络仅用于：通过 StoreKit 获取 App Store 商品信息、校验或恢复购买，以及在你点击链接时用浏览器打开本政策或用户协议。

七、无跟踪、无广告

App 不包含广告、统计或第三方跟踪 SDK，我们不会出售或共享个人数据。

八、保存与删除

数据会一直保存在你的设备上，直到你主动删除。你可以在设置中清空票箱；删除 App 会一并移除其本地数据，包括归档的原件和 App 目录内的导出文件。

九、儿童

本 App 不面向 13 周岁以下儿童。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息——事实上，我们不收集任何个人信息。
